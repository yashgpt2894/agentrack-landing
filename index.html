Six ways to stop flying blind on AI.

Built for enterprises that already have agents everywhere — not for the ones still deciding whether to build them.

  • C-01

    Agent discovery

    Automatically finds every agent running across your org — built on Claude, GPT, Gemini, or custom frameworks. No manual registration required.

  • C-02

    Redundancy detection

    Semantic analysis surfaces overlapping agents across teams — like three departments each running their own meeting summarizer — and quantifies the duplicate spend.

  • C-03

    Scope drift monitoring

    Continuously checks whether agents still do what they were built to do — and flags drift before it becomes a compliance or cost problem.

  • C-04

    Cost attribution

    Maps LLM token usage to teams, agents and use cases. Your AI bill stops being a black box and becomes a line-item breakdown.

  • C-05

    Agent lineage map

    A live graph of how agents interact, what data they touch, and which business processes depend on them. Your org’s AI nervous system, mapped.

  • C-06

    EU AI Act readiness

    Auto-generates the audit trail and documentation your compliance team needs for the August 2026 high-risk obligations. Built for EMEA enterprises.
